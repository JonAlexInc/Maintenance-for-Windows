# Community conduct

Treat participants respectfully. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation in project spaces. Apply these standards consistently and consider good-faith mistakes proportionately.

A private conduct reporting contact must be established before opening this project to public participation. Do not post private complaints or personal information in public issues. This local preview is not yet accepting external contributions.
