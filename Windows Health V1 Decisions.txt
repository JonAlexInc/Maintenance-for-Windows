Maintenance for Windows — Version One Decisions
Approved baseline as of October 10, 2026

STATUS
The user approved the version one direction and pay-once Technician model. The product naming scheme is approved. Visual design, pricing, support term, and post-service access model remain proposals. This is a project record, not a claim of implemented software.

LOCKED PRODUCT DIRECTION
Free personal Windows health application, simple enough for everyday users with a free expert view. Paid Technician edition adds professional workflows around the same cleanup and diagnostic engine. Core safety, evidence, exclusions, and basic action records are not paid upgrades.
Product promise: see what changed, clean up with confidence, and keep a clear record of results.
Initial platform: Windows 11 x64. Initial checks are on demand, local, account-free, and usable offline.

PERSONAL 1.0 SCOPE
Prioritized overview with timestamps, check coverage, unknown states, and next actions; no arbitrary health score.
Conservative cleanup of known user and Windows temporary locations, preview, exclusions, in-use handling, and execution-time validation.
Small published list of tested application caches; initial browser candidates Chrome, Edge, Firefox. Preserve credentials, cookies, sessions, history, and offline content by default.
Separate explicit Recycle Bin review and empty action.
Local drive capacity/free space and supported cleanup-category sizes; no full-drive index.
Compare snapshots of tracked storage locations, application inventory, and supported startup entries. Distinguish observation from attribution and cause.
Startup inspection and links to Windows controls; direct startup modifications deferred.
Basic low-space, available protection-status, and pending-restart checks; inaccessible information remains unknown.
Local scan summaries, action history, retention controls, preferences, exclusions, and dismissed recommendations.
Free expert details and basic readable support export with preview and redaction options.
Deletion is not universally reversible. Report recoverability honestly and distinguish retained bytes from reclaimed space.

TECHNICIAN 1.0 SCOPE
Service sessions with client/machine reference, symptoms, and notes.
Baseline and final comparisons; distinguish app-executed actions from externally observed changes.
Reusable profiles that prepare plans without silently authorizing actions or overriding safety rules.
Client-ready branded PDF reports, structured exports, and local searchable session archive on the technician's computer.
Portable operation with explicit job-data destination; no technician license or unrelated client records left on customer machines.
Workflow: open job, capture baseline, review findings, execute selected cleanup, recheck, deliver report.

LOCKED COMMERCIAL MODEL
Personal edition is free. Technician is purchased once per major version, not a mandatory subscription. Purchased major version does not expire. All released 1.x updates are included for 1.x owners. Version 2 and later major upgrades are optional paid purchases. Skipping a major version does not disable the existing license.
Baseline licensing design: one named technician may service multiple client PCs. Offline license operation. Client may retain the free personal application.
Permanent use does not promise indefinite support or future Windows compatibility. Publish the support period and supported Windows versions before sales. Price and upgrade discount remain undecided.

LATER
More tested rules and refinements in 1.x. Scheduled checks, growth alerts, reversible startup controls after core validation. Major guided repairs and deeper crash/update diagnosis may justify 2.0. Separate validation tracks for malware, rootkits, offline remediation, ARM64, older Windows support, Mac, Linux, remote access, and hosted fleet services.
No current commitment to registry cleaning, RAM boosting, automatic driver replacement, bulk service disabling, or fix-everything actions.

RELEASE ACCEPTANCE
Respect exclusions and intended paths despite concurrent file changes. Skip uncertain items with explanation. Confirm browser defaults preserve sessions. Record successes, failures, skips, and observed changes accurately. Avoid false reclaimed-space precision. Keep technician credentials and archive off client PCs. Validate offline workflow and comprehension with representative users.

PROPOSED POST SERVICE DESIGN NOT YET APPROVED
Separate Personal and Technician workspaces using the shared engine. Expert view is not Technician authorization.
Technician workspace requires a valid license plus local technician authentication; app authorization is separate from Windows privilege elevation.
On session lock, inactivity, close, or restart, privileged technician actions require fresh authorization. Pending operations must finish safely or cancel safely rather than being interrupted blindly.
End-service flow previews client deliverables, saves the technician archive externally, closes authorization, and optionally leaves the personal app plus this client's approved report and exclusions.
Optional Guided Care leaves the client ordinary checks, report access, and an approved cleanup profile, while protecting profile edits and advanced actions from accidental changes. No background remote control.
PC owner can remove Guided Care through a clear local recovery/reset route. Personal history and reports remain accessible. A local administrator can ultimately alter local software; this is not enterprise tamper-proof lockdown.
No shared master PIN, resident technician-wide license secret, unrelated client archive, or hidden support connection on the client machine.
True OS lockdown, kiosk mode, and organization-enforced policy are outside 1.0.

NAMING AND DESIGN
Approved product names: Maintenance for Windows; Maintenance for Mac; Maintenance for Linux. Paid edition naming: Maintenance for Windows — Technician Edition, with the same pattern for future platforms. Proposed GitHub slug: maintenance-for-windows. Prior naming candidates are superseded. Version 0.01 implementation scope is recorded in docs/V0.01.md; the wider V1 scope remains planned.
Visual direction proposal: calm native Windows layout; persistent Overview, Cleanup, Changes, History navigation; expandable technical detail; distinct authorized Technician workspace and explicit end-service handoff. No warning-heavy dashboard or performance score.
